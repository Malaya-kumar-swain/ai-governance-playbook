# 6. Vendor and model due diligence

Ask these before signing with an LLM provider or AI vendor. Ask for evidence, not only answers.

## Data

1. Do you store our prompts and outputs? For how long, and where (which countries)?
2. Do you use our data to train or improve any model? Can we switch that off in the contract?
3. Who at your company, or at your sub-processors, can see our data?
4. How do you delete our data on request, and how do you prove it?
5. Can inference and storage stay in India if we need that?

## Security

6. Which independent certifications or audits do you hold, and can we see the reports?
7. How do you protect against prompt injection and data leakage in your product?
8. How will you notify us of a security incident, and how fast?

## Quality and safety

9. Which languages do you support well, and what evidence do you have for Indian languages?
10. What safety testing do you do before releasing a new model version?
11. Will you notify us before changing or retiring the model we depend on?

## Commercial and continuity

12. How is usage priced, and what are the rate limits at our expected volume?
13. What is your uptime commitment, and what happens if you miss it?
14. Can we export our data, prompts, fine-tunes and configurations if we leave?
15. Who owns outputs and any fine-tuned models built on our data?

## Red flags

- No clear answer on training with customer data
- No way to keep data in India when your sector requires it
- No advance notice of model changes
- No evidence for claims about Indian-language quality
